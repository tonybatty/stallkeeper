# Floors are private to the owner, not banned from every browser

The original rule said floors never reach the browser, but the owner has to review suggested floors and correct saved ones. A logged-in owner may read and edit floors. Buyer responses, model prompts, printed tags and public feeds never contain them. We claim only that the AI stallholder cannot agree a price below the floor, not that a floor can never be inferred, because repeated offers can reveal a bound.
